The read

    Written by Claude after each weekly upload. The scores below are calculated; this is commentary.

    Where the points are

    Strength

    Each lift is worth 5 points, split by effective % gain on estimated 1RM (best 3–10 rep set; reps for leg raises). The bar runs from each person's baseline: the lighter stretch up to their previous best only counts at half value. Latest = best of the most recent two weeks.

    Efficiency test

    Average power over the final 15 minutes of a 20-minute hold at a fixed heart rate. Baseline week of 5 Oct, scored final test week of 7 Dec.

    Recovery

    Steps floor

    Penalty only: at least 56,000 steps each Mon–Sun week (Garmin weekly total). Each missed week costs 1 point, up to 10. Deload weeks count; a declared wildcard week is exempt. Steps never earn points.
